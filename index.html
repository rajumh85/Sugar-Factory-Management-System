Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our organic certification, farm payouts, and shipping process.

How is SugarGreen different from supermarket white sugar?

Commercial sugar is bleached with toxic sulfur dioxide and stripped of all micro-nutrients through chemical refining. SugarGreen products are unbleached, cold-clarified using natural organic mucilage, and retain active iron, calcium, magnesium, and natural enzymes.

How does SugarGreen ensure fair prices for rural farmers?

By connecting farmers directly to consumers through our digital platform, we eliminate commission-hungry brokers. Over 80% of every sale goes directly to the sugarcane grower's bank account, supporting rural livelihood and green farming education.

What is the shelf life and recommended storage for jaggery and syrup?

Our traditional jaggery and sugarcubes stay fresh for up to 12 months when stored in a cool, dry, airtight container. Pure sugarcane syrup and molasses should be refrigerated after opening to preserve their delicate floral notes.

Can businesses place bulk or wholesale orders?

Yes! We supply cafes, artisanal sweet-makers, wellness centres, and wedding event caterers with custom packaging and wholesale pricing. Contact us at support@sugargreen.in or call +91 9113963499 for dedicated B2B quotes.

What are the shipping charges and delivery timelines?

Orders above ₹499 enjoy 100% Free Doorstep Delivery. Karnataka deliveries typically arrive within 24 to 48 hours, while all-India deliveries take 3 to 5 business days with live SMS and WhatsApp tracking.
